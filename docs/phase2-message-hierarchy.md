## Homepage: 5-Second / 30-Second Audit

**What does the visitor understand in 5 seconds?**
- This is a marketing consultancy in GCC markets.
- They believe growth sometimes needs better decisions, not more activity.
- They offer two paths: Consulting and Execution.

**What does the visitor understand in 30 seconds?**
- Elite Maison diagnoses before executing.
- They use a Four I framework (Insight, Ideas, Influence, Impact).
- They have 18+ years of GCC experience and 5 approved case studies.
- The next step is to ask a question (لديك استفسار؟ / Have a question?), which opens the Contact Dock directly.

**What important question is still unanswered?**
- What happens after I send an inquiry? — no timeline, no process description, no what to expect copy.
- Which path is right for me? — equal visual weight makes this unclear.
- What kind of client do you work with? — no explicit situation-matching.

**What proof appears before it is needed?**
- 18+ years and GCC focus appear before the visitor has decided the firm understands their specific problem.
- Generic credential proof (years, regions) appears before outcome proof (case metrics).

**What proof appears too late?**
- Case metrics appear after the Four I's and delivery path, which are method proof, not outcome proof.
- The strongest proof (case metrics) is buried below the method explanation.

**What wording is generic?**
- Growth and marketing consultancy (category label)
- 18+ years of hands-on experience (table stakes)
- GCC markets (geographic descriptor without mental availability)
- Start a conversation (overused CTA)

**What wording is uniquely Elite Maison?**
- *Historical:* Growth does not always need more marketing. Sometimes it needs a better decision. *(superseded by owner-approved hero 2026-09-20)*
- *Current:* Greater clarity. Better decisions. Stronger growth.
- Four I's framework (Insight, Ideas, Influence, Impact)
- Diagnose -> Prioritize -> Execute -> Improve (method shorthand)
- From the work (proof ledger eyebrow)
- Ideas meant to change a decision, not fill a blog (insight positioning)
- Tell us what is happening. We will start from there. (closing CTA)
