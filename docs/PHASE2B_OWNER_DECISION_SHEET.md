## Questions That Must Be Answered Before Phase 2B

> **Maximum 5 genuinely blocking questions.**

| # | Question | Why it blocks Phase 2B |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the minimum engagement size (budget range, duration, scope)? | Copy cannot describe what the visitor is committing to without this. Blocks CP-06 (next-step framing) and any offer-related copy. |
| 2 | What is the pricing model (retainer, project, daily rate, success fee, hybrid)? | Shapes value proposition, objection handling, and CTA framing. Current copy avoids pricing entirely. |
| 3 | What exactly happens after a visitor submits the inquiry form? What is the timeline and next step? | Directly blocks CP-06 (Contact Page Next-Step Timeline). The biggest unanswered question on the contact page. |
| 4 | What is the boundary between consulting and execution? Can a client engage in one without the other? | Blocks CP-03 (Two Paths Decision Aid) and CP-04 (About Page Situation Matcher). Visitor needs to know which path to choose first. |
| 5 | What does a typical consulting engagement look like (duration, deliverables, cost range)? | Blocks CP-04 and CP-06. Helps visitor self-qualify and reduces sales friction. |
