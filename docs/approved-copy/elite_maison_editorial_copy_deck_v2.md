# 0. Editorial rules

1. Arabic and English are authored independently. Do not literal-translate one into the other.
2. Do not invent clients, dates, locations, awards, response times, team size, certifications, revenue, performance percentages, or capabilities not already supported by the repository.
3. The following factual claims remain locked:
   - More than 18 years of hands-on experience.
   - GCC market focus / experience.
   - Attractive Smile: 12 consecutive days of bookings; current approved non-quantified revenue growth / steadier demand wording only.
   - Bloom / Perfect Foodstuff: AED 18K in Q1 → AED 40K → average AED 65K/month.
   - Bin Ablan: Canada, Oman, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Libya; five-country market entry; new distribution channels.
   - Le Patchouli Café: 11 franchise branches; scalable/repeatable model.
   - AI Brains: clear/applicable/measurable project; “Award for Best AI-Supporting Project.” Do not add awarding body/year/rank.
4. SEO/GEO must be protected through page structure, metadata, semantic headings, answerability, internal linking and crawlable HTML — not by making every paragraph sound like a keyword target.
5. No keyword stuffing, fake FAQ blocks, city pages, bot-only text, invisible keyword blocks, or duplicated “SEO copy”.
6. The final tone should feel experienced, commercial, intellectually confident, human, concise enough to scan and rich enough to keep reading.
7. Prefer tension and specificity over generic consultancy language.
8. Do not use “Book a consultation” unless a real Cal.com booking experience is configured and working. If Cal.com is not configured, hide that booking action rather than faking it.
