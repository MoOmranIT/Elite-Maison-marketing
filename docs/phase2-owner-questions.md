# Phase 2A — Owner Questions

**Document version:** v1  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-20  
**Purpose:** Consolidate only the questions that materially block better copy/CRO. Maximum 12 questions. Prioritized by highest-value missing inputs.

---

## Commercial Model (P0)

| # | Question | Why it matters | Category |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the minimum engagement size (budget range, duration, scope)? | Sets visitor expectation and filters unqualified inquiries. Without this, copy cannot describe what the visitor is committing to. | Commercial model |
| 2 | What is the pricing model (retainer, project, daily rate, success fee, hybrid)? | Shapes value proposition, objection handling, and CTA framing. Current copy avoids pricing entirely, which may increase uncertainty for some visitors. | Commercial model |

---

## Inquiry Process (P0)

| # | Question | Why it matters | Category |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | What exactly happens after a visitor submits the inquiry form? What is the timeline and next step? | What happens after I submit? is the biggest unanswered question on the contact page. A clear next-step description reduces commitment friction and improves form completion. | Inquiry process |

---

## Engagement Model (P1)

| # | Question | Why it matters | Category |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | What is the boundary between consulting and execution? Can a client engage in one without the other? | The two paths are presented as linked but not as sequential. Visitor may not know if they need consulting first, execution first, or both. | Engagement model |
| 5 | What does a typical consulting engagement look like (duration, deliverables, cost range)? | Helps visitor self-qualify and reduces sales friction. Current copy describes capabilities but not engagement shape. | Engagement model |
| 6 | What does a typical execution engagement look like (duration, deliverables, cost range)? | Same as above, for execution path. | Engagement model |

---

## Positioning (P1)

| # | Question | Why it matters | Category |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | What kind of client should NOT contact Elite Maison? | Sets expectations and reduces wrong-audience inquiries. A clear disqualifier improves inquiry quality and brand perception. | Positioning |
| 8 | What do clients most often misunderstand about Elite Maison before they contact? | Reveals messaging gaps and objections that copy can address proactively. | Positioning |

---

## Decision-Maker Profile (P1)

| # | Question | Why it matters | Category |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Who are the named decision-makers and what are their exact titles/roles? | Personas and messaging must speak to real buyers, not inferred roles. Current copy addresses leaders generically. | Audience |
| 10 | What verbatim language do clients use when describing their problem before they contact Elite Maison? | Customer language drives copy resonance and answerability. Current copy uses consultant voice, not customer voice. | Audience |

## Competitive Set (P2)

| # | Question | Why it matters | Category |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | Which named competitors does the owner consider primary? | Competitive positioning and differentiation must be grounded in real competitive set. Current positioning names activity-first agencies as the alternative but does not name specific competitors. | Positioning |
