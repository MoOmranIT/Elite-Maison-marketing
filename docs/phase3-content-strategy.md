# Phase 3A — Content Strategy
**Status:** AUDIT ONLY — no implementation changes  
**Date:** 2026-09-20  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Elite Maison has credibility to become known for **practical GCC growth decision-making** — the intersection of diagnostic clarity, commercial strategy, and execution discipline. This is distinctive because most competitors separate strategy from execution, and most GCC-focused advisors focus on entry/legal/tax rather than marketing/commercial readiness.

Recommended pillars: 3 (not 5) — quality over quantity.

| Content Type | Classification | Rationale |
|--------------|---------------|-----------|
| Growth diagnosis framework | BOTH | Captures search demand; framework is shareable |
| GCC expansion readiness | BOTH | Search demand exists; checklist is highly shareable |
| Sector briefs | BOTH | Sector-specific search demand; shareable in sector communities |
| Practical guides | SEARCHABLE | How-to intent, long-tail capture |
| Analytical articles | BOTH | Thought leadership, AI citation potential |
| Case insights | SHAREABLE | Proof-driven, social proof in sector networks |
